Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website (saintlife.dev), how it is used, and your rights in relation to it. This site is a personal portfolio operated by Jonathan Kariuki.

This site does not use tracking scripts, third-party analytics, or advertising. The information collected is limited to what is strictly necessary to respond to direct contact.

2. Information Collected

This site collects the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information — when you email via the contact links on this site, your email address and the content of your message are received. This is standard email communication and occurs outside this website (via your email client).
  • Server logs — the hosting provider (Vercel) may automatically log standard request data including IP address, browser type, referring page, and time of visit. This is handled by Vercel under their own Privacy Policy.

3. How Information Is Used

Contact information you provide is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or message
  • Follow up on project discussions or proposals

It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for unsolicited marketing.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This site uses a single item of localStorage (not a cookie) to remember your dark/light theme preference across visits. This value (theme) stays on your device and is never transmitted to any server. No tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels are used.

5. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used and may process limited data as part of normal page loading:

  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log the request including your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — this site is hosted on Vercel. Server-side logs are governed by Vercel's Privacy Policy.

No other third-party scripts, trackers, or services are loaded by this site.

6. Data Retention

Emails received are retained only as long as necessary to complete the relevant conversation or project engagement. There is no automated system storing your data — messages are managed manually via a standard email inbox.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data held about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact:

saintlife54@gmail.com

Requests will be responded to within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, reach out at saintlife54@gmail.com.
