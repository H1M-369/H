Service · AI agents

AI agents that take work off your desk.

An AI agent handles one repetitive task end to end: reading your documents, deciding what to do, and producing the reply or report. It works from your information, not guesses.

What an agent can take on

Customer support replies

Reads each customer email, finds the answer in your help docs, and drafts a reply that cites its source.

Research and reports

Looks at a business and its competitors and returns a scored report with a list of what to fix first.

Answering from your documents

Policies, price lists or product guides become something staff and customers can ask questions of.

How it works
  1. Pick one taskWe agree on one job the agent does, what it reads, and what it hands back.
  2. Connect your sourcesYour documents, inbox or tools, so answers come from your information.
  3. Test on real inputsIt is checked against real emails and edge cases before it goes live.
  4. Run and reviewEvery run is logged, so you can see what it did and why.
Questions
Will it make things up?

It is built to answer only from the documents you give it, and to show where each answer came from.

Does it replace my staff?

It takes the repetitive part of a task, so your team can spend time on the parts that need a person.

How much does an agent cost?

Agents are quoted per project, once the task is scoped. You get a clear quote before any work starts.
