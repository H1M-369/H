Questions
What is a static website?

A site made of fixed pages, such as home, services, about and contact. With no database to maintain, it loads fast and costs less to run, which makes it the right fit for most small businesses.

How much does a website cost?

Simple static websites start at KSh 30,000. Full e-commerce stores start at KSh 250,000. You get a clear quote before any work starts.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first, since most visitors in Kenya browse on their phones.

Can customers pay by M-Pesa?

Yes. M-Pesa and card payments are part of every e-commerce build.

Can you redesign or speed up my current site?

Yes. A redesign or refactor is quoted after a short review of the site you have now.
