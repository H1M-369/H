Service · Automation

Automations that run while you work.

An automation connects the tools you already use and runs a routine task on its own, on a schedule or whenever something arrives, so nobody has to copy, paste or remember.

What you can automate

Email replies

New support emails are read, answered from your policies and replied to in the same thread, around the clock.

Daily and weekly reports

News, figures or updates gathered, summarised and sent to your inbox at the same time every day.

Record keeping

Every run is written to Google Sheets, so there is a log you can check and share.

How it works
  1. Map the routineWe write down the steps you repeat today, what triggers them and where the result goes.
  2. Connect the toolsGmail, Google Sheets and the other services involved are linked securely.
  3. Handle the exceptionsSpam, missing data and failures are caught, retried or flagged to you.
  4. Run and logIt runs on its own, and every run is recorded so you can check it.
Questions
Which tools can it connect to?

Gmail and Google Sheets are shown in the demos. Other tools are checked during scoping.

What happens if something fails?

Failures are retried or flagged to you, and every run is logged, so nothing disappears silently.

How much does an automation cost?

Automations are quoted per project, once the task is scoped. You get a clear quote before any work starts.
