Ember & Oak — Restaurant Site

A restaurant site that turns visitors into table bookings.

A restaurant website that works well on phones: a menu with photos of every dish, dietary labels, a photo gallery, opening hours, and table bookings with an instant confirmation.

  1. Tabbed menuMenu sections switch in place, and hovering a dish shows its photo.
  2. Dietary labelsChef's pick, vegan and gluten-free marked on each dish.
  3. Reservation formName, day, time and party size in one short form.
  4. ConfirmationThe guest sees straight away that their table is booked.

Questions about Ember & Oak.

How much would a site like this cost?

A business website like this starts at KSh 60,000, with hosting billed at cost.

Can table bookings come straight to me?

Yes. In a real build, each reservation can be sent to your email or WhatsApp as soon as a guest books.

Can the menu show photos of the dishes?

Yes. On this site, hovering a dish shows its photo, and on phones each dish shows a small thumbnail.
