Email Autoresponder Pipeline

Event-driven Gmail pipeline with no human in the loop: poll, parse and filter, retrieve the matching policy docs, draft with Claude, then reply in-thread and log the run.

  1. PollChecks for unread mail every 30 seconds.
  2. Parse & filterExtracts sender, subject and intent, and drops newsletters and spam.
  3. RetrieveLoads the support policy that matches the intent.
  4. DraftClaude writes a reply constrained to those docs.
  5. SendReplies in-thread, marks the message read and logs the run.
