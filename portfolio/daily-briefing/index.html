Daily Briefing Pipeline

Your news and weather, summarised and in your inbox every morning.

Scheduled six-stage pipeline: headlines for chosen topics plus local weather, compressed by Claude into two-sentence summaries, emailed as a digest and logged to Google Sheets on every run.

  1. Cron triggerFires daily at 07:00, or manually on demand.
  2. Fetch newsPulls headlines for the configured topics.
  3. Fetch weatherCurrent conditions and the day's forecast.
  4. SummariseClaude compresses each story to two sentences.
  5. Email digestRenders the briefing and sends it to your inbox.
  6. Log runAppends the date, topics and item count to Sheets.
