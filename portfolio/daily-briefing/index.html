Daily Briefing

Your news and weather, summarised and in your inbox every morning.

An automation that runs every morning. It collects the news on your chosen topics and the weather, has AI shorten each story to two sentences, emails you the summary, and keeps a record in Google Sheets.

  1. Starts each morningRuns at 7:00 AM every day, or whenever you ask.
  2. Gets the newsCollects headlines on the topics you chose.
  3. Gets the weatherToday's conditions and forecast.
  4. SummarisesAI shortens each story to two sentences.
  5. Emails youThe summary lands in your inbox.
  6. Keeps a recordAdds a line to Google Sheets each day.

About this workflow

Questions about the Daily Briefing.

Can I choose the topics and the time?

Yes. You pick the news topics and the city for the weather, and it runs at a set time each morning or whenever you ask for it.

Where does the briefing go?

It's emailed to you as a formatted digest, and every run is logged as a row in Google Sheets.

How much does a workflow like this cost?

A single workflow starts at KSh 8,000 setup, then the actual usage cost plus a management plan each month.
