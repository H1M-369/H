Questions about Competitor Analysis.

What does the report include?

An overall score out of 100, what's working, what isn't, how you compare with the competitors you name, and a list of next steps ranked by impact.

Is the analysis in the demo real?

No. The demo uses a fictional business and sample findings to show how the report is laid out.

A custom AI agent starts at KSh 25,000 setup, then the actual AI usage cost plus a management plan each month.
