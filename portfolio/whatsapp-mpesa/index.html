WhatsApp + M-Pesa Store

Get paid by M-Pesa and see every order on WhatsApp the moment it's paid.

An online store wired to Safaricom's Daraja API for M-Pesa STK-push payments and to the WhatsApp Business Platform for owner alerts. An order is only confirmed once Safaricom's callback verifies the payment.

  1. CheckoutThe customer picks items and enters their M-Pesa number.
  2. STK pushDaraja sends a payment prompt straight to the customer's phone.
  3. ConfirmThe customer enters their M-Pesa PIN, with no paybill number to type.
  4. CallbackSafaricom confirms the payment and the store records the receipt.
  5. WhatsApp alertThe owner gets the order, receipt and delivery details on WhatsApp.
