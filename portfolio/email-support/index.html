Custom Agent

Email Support Agent

Answers customer emails from your own help docs, so your team doesn't have to.

Retrieval-grounded reply agent. It takes your support docs and an inbound email, locates the section that answers the question, and drafts a reply restricted to that source, with the citation shown.

  1. Ingest docsPaste text or upload a .txt knowledge base.
  2. Inbound emailPaste the customer's message verbatim.
  3. RetrieveThe agent matches the question to the relevant doc section.
  4. Cited replyA send-ready draft, with the source section attached.
