Agent Workflow

Email Autoresponder Pipeline

A five-step pipeline that answers support email automatically. It watches Gmail, filters out noise, reads your support docs, drafts a reply with Claude, and sends it in the same thread.

  1. WatchChecks the inbox for new unread mail every 30 seconds.
  2. Parse & filterPulls out the sender, subject and intent, and skips newsletters and spam.
  3. Load docsFetches the support policy that matches the question.
  4. DraftClaude writes a reply based only on those docs.
  5. SendReplies in the same thread, marks the email read, and logs it.

Agent Workflow

Daily Briefing Pipeline

A morning digest that assembles itself. It runs on a schedule, pulls headlines for your topics and the weather, has Claude summarise everything, emails it to you, and logs each run to Google Sheets.

  1. Scheduled triggerRuns every day at 7:00 AM, or on demand.
  2. Fetch newsGets headlines for the topics you picked.
  3. Fetch weatherGets current conditions and today's forecast.
  4. SummariseClaude writes a two-sentence summary of each story.
  5. Email digestThe formatted briefing arrives in your inbox.
  6. Log to SheetsAdds a row with the date, topics and item count.
