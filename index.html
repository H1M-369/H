How I work

From spec
to production.

01
Define the output
Pin down what done looks like, whether that's the page, the reply or the report, before choosing a stack.
02
Build the thin path
Wire the smallest working version end to end first, then add features only where the output needs them.
03
Test on real inputs
Sites across breakpoints and browsers. Agents against real emails and malformed data, not happy-path samples.
04
Ship, then instrument
Deploy early, log every run, and iterate on what the logs show rather than on guesses.

FAQ

Before you ask.

How much does a website cost?

Business websites start at KSh 30,000. A full e-commerce store starts at KSh 250,000. Agents and automations are quoted per project, once the task is scoped. You get a clear quote before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. The quote comes with a timeline, so you know the delivery date before you commit.

Can you add M-Pesa and WhatsApp?

Yes. A WhatsApp chat button can go on any site, and M-Pesa and card payments are part of every e-commerce build. The WhatsApp + M-Pesa demo shows a paid order arriving on the owner's WhatsApp.

What would an AI agent actually do for my business?

It takes one repetitive task off your hands. For example, it can answer customer emails from your own help docs, send you a morning briefing, or report on your competitors. Each demo on this site shows one running.

Can you fix or speed up my current website?

Yes. A refactor fixes broken layouts and accessibility problems and speeds up slow pages. It is quoted after a short review of the site you have now.
