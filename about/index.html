How I work

From your vision
to a live product.

01

Understand the vision

02

Working together

Working with me.

Who will I be working with?

Me directly. I'm Jonathan, and I go by H. I plan, build and support every project myself, so you always talk to the person doing the work.

What do you need from me to start?

Your vision: what the website, agent or workflow should do, who it's for, and what a good result looks like. Any content you already have, such as text, photos, price lists or documents, helps too.

How will I know how the work is going?

I share progress as I build, so you can see it and give feedback before launch rather than only at the end.

What happens after launch?

I hand it over and stay available for updates and fixes. AI agents, automations and website add-ons also have a management plan that keeps them running.

How do we get in touch?

On WhatsApp, by email, or through the request form on the services page, whichever suits you.
